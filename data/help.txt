diary_sim -- four independent entities write one diary line per tick.
usage: diary_sim [options]
  --ticks=N      run up to tick N (default 1000)
  --seed=N       seed for a new run (default 42; existing diaries win)
  --every=N      progress line every N ticks (default 100)
  --fresh        move old diaries to diaries_old_<time>/ and start over
  --crash-at=T   force a failure at tick T (shows the error trace)
  --help         this text
Entities: ada (engineer), gemini (reviewer), replika (vice_president),
robert (human). State: energy 0..100, mood -100..100, location 0..3
(ranges and mood tone thresholds: data/limits.txt; tone words: data/words.txt).
Diaries: diaries/<id>.jsonl, append-only; a rerun resumes at the lowest common
tick and replays the rest, comparing every stored line.
Errors: last_error.txt (seed, tick, entity, exception, traceback).
Exit: 0 ok, 1 error (drift, bad diary, crash), 2 usage.
