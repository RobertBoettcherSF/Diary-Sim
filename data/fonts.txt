-- The block-letter font of the main menu title (Sim_Menu), in three sizes:
-- [small] 3 wide x 5 tall, [medium] 5 x 5, [large] 7 x 7 (data/menu.txt
-- title.size picks one).  Each glyph is a "glyph = C" line (C is one
-- character, or the word space) followed by its rows, top first, one a line
-- between two | marks: inside them any non-space character is ink and a
-- space is blank.  Rows are exactly as wide as the size's cell.  Needed for
-- the title letters only (A-Z, 0-9, space and . , ! ? - : ' /); lowercase is
-- drawn as the capital, any other character as '?'.  Missing or broken: the
-- title is shown as plain spaced letters.  Lines starting with "--" are comments.
