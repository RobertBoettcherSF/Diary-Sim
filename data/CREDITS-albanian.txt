Credits of the Albanian data (build 0.064, alb=1 sessions; built by scripts/make_albanian_data.py, maintainer-only)

data/sq_book.txt
  Sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), English-Albanian pairs, licensed under CC BY 2.0 FR
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).  Every line keeps the Tatoeba sentence id of the Albanian sentence
  (first column), so each sentence can be traced to its contributors at https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>.
  Changes: sentences written without the letters e-diaeresis / c-cedilla (stripped diacritics) and sentences with a known
  stripped word (nje, eshte, jane, shum, per, qe, mire, ...) were left out; the text is otherwise unchanged.

data/sq_nouns.txt, data/sq_noun_forms.txt
  Derived from the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org) through the machine-readable extract of kaikki.org
  (https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Albanian/; Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract", LREC 2022).  Wiktionary text is licensed under
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation License.
  Changes: only nouns with a recorded gender, the lemma, gender, plural and definite forms; stress accents removed; trimmed.
  SHARE-ALIKE: these two derived files are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well; if you change and share them, share
  them under the same licence and keep this credit.  The rest of diary-sim is not affected by this licence.

data/sq_adj.txt and data/wesen_gender.txt are written for diary-sim.
